Cute Happy Frog Dot-to-Dot Puzzle Guide

Ribbit! Here comes a happy little frog with round, bulging eyes and a big cheerful grin. This 82-dot puzzle is a bit longer than most, making it a great next step for kids who have mastered the basics and are ready for a bigger challenge. Grab a pencil, take it section by section, and watch this cheerful hopper leap onto the page!

1–20 — The Big Round Eyes

Start at dot 1 and work through to dot 20 to draw the frog's two big, round eyes sitting on top of its head. This is a longer opening section, so encourage children to move steadily and check the dot numbers often rather than rushing.

💡
Fun fact!
A frog's eyes sit high on its head so it can hide almost its whole body underwater while still watching for danger and dinner above the surface.

21–42 — The Smiling Face

Continue from dot 21 to dot 42 to shape the frog's wide, cheerful mouth and rounded face. Lots of small curves appear here, so remind kids to look one dot ahead before drawing each line to keep the smile smooth.

💡
Fun fact!
Frogs don't chew their food — most swallow prey whole, using their eyes to help push it down their throat!

43–64 — The Bumpy Body

Trace dots 43 through 64 to draw the frog's round, bumpy body. The lines here are a little longer, giving kids a chance to practise smoother, more confident strokes after all the small curves in the face.

💡
Fun fact!
Some frogs can jump more than 20 times their own body length in a single leap — that's like a human jumping the length of a basketball court!

65–82 — The Springy Legs

Finish by connecting dots 65 through 82 to add the frog's strong, springy back legs and little front feet. Just eighteen dots to go — take your time on this final stretch so the legs look ready to hop!

💡
Fun fact!
A frog's powerful back legs store energy like a spring, letting it launch into a jump almost instantly whenever it senses danger.
